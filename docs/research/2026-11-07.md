## 2. Specs

- virtio 1.4 was "expected soon" as of Dec 2025 ([vmsplice](https://blog.vmsplice.net/2025/12/)); I could not confirm publication. Net-relevant drafts from the 1.4 requirements ([thread](https://lists-archive.oasis-open.org/archive/lists/virtio/months/202306/messages/44)): notification coalescing, receive flow filters, header data split, packet timestamps, low-latency queues. None is required by an SNP driver; notification coalescing and header data split are only loosely related to #3 and must stay off by default.
- Nothing found changing UEFI SNP.

## 3. Open issues

- #4 (jumbo, P2): the spec route is already in the issue: `VIRTIO_NET_F_MTU` (`config.mtu`), receive buffers of at least mtu + header, or `VIRTIO_NET_F_MRG_RXBUF` mergeable buffers. No new prior art found beyond that.
- #3 (throughput, P3): spec-level levers to measure: batch RX refill and kick once per batch, suppress used-buffer interrupts/notifications with `VIRTIO_F_EVENT_IDX` or avail flags (polling driver, so interrupts can stay off), `VIRTIO_F_IN_ORDER` if the device offers it, larger queues than 32. Nothing external to cite.
